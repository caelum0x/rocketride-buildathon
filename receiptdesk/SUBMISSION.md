## 60-second demo script

0:00 — "Every small business does this: a shoebox of receipts, retyped into a
spreadsheet once a month." Open the app on staging.
0:08 — Click **Load sample** (or drop real receipt photos). "Snap them or paste the
text — one or many."
0:18 — Click **Generate**. While it runs: "It OCRs each receipt and pulls out date,
merchant, category, and amount."
0:30 — Scroll the result: the **Expenses** table, then the **Summary** with per-category
and grand totals. "Categorized and totalled — ready for the books."
0:45 — Click **Download .md**. "Straight into their accounting." Then: "‹your business
owner› ran ‹their real receipts› through it and ‹what happened›."
0:57 — "A pile of receipts in, an expense sheet out. That's Receipt Desk." End on the
launch link.
