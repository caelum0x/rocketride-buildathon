## 60-second demo script

0:00 — "Most study time is spent *making* notes, not learning them. Study Pack does the
making." Open the app on staging.
0:08 — Click **Load sample** (or paste a real lecture / drop a slide photo). "Any
lecture — transcript, notes, or a photo of the board."
0:18 — Click **Generate study pack**. While it runs: "It OCRs images, then structures
everything into summary, key concepts, detailed notes, and practice questions."
0:30 — Scroll the result: Summary → Key Concepts → Practice Questions → Answer Key.
"Exam-ready, grounded only in the material — it won't invent facts."
0:45 — Click **Download .md**. "Keep it, share it." Then: "‹your classmate› used it for
‹their exam› and ‹what happened›."
0:57 — "One lecture in, a study pack out. That's Study Pack." End on the launch link.
